4. Privacy policy

What data is collected?

Only what you enter in the contact form: your name, your email address and your message. No data is collected without your knowledge.

What is it used for?

Only to reply to you and, if you wish, to prepare a quote. It is never sold, rented out or used for advertising.

Who processes it?

The data controller is Cino Alessio (contact details above). The form is sent through the Web3Forms service, which forwards your message by email. The website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is passed to Google during your visit. If you contact me via WhatsApp, the conversation goes through that service (Meta) and is subject to its own terms.

How long is it kept?

For as long as our exchange requires, then for at most 3 years after our last contact, unless we have a contract (in which case the legal retention period for accounting documents applies).

Cookies

This website uses no advertising cookies and no audience measurement tools. It only remembers, in your browser, your choice of light or dark theme and whether you've already seen the intro animation. It also keeps a copy of the pages you've visited so they remain available offline. This information never leaves your device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can at any time request access to, correction of or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to cinoalessio@outlook.com. If you feel your rights are not being respected, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (DPA), Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels — dataprotectionauthority.be.
