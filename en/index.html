Beauty & wellbeing · Figma prototype, mobile & desktop

Ô Natur'Elle — A complete prototype for a beauty salon

Ô Natur'Elle is a premium beauty salon founded by a qualified beautician. For this real client project, I designed a complete, responsive Figma prototype from start to finish, with one guiding idea: help an undecided client find the right treatment and book it effortlessly — treatment showcase, personalised beauty diagnosis, 3-step booking, shop and gallery.

  • Scope: 30+ screens, in mobile and desktop versions
  • Key feature: a 4-question diagnosis that recommends the right treatment
  • Flow: 3-step booking with an animated confirmation

02
Local retail · Figma prototype + working code, mobile

Belvantage — a shared loyalty card for independent shops

A personal project, thought through end to end: a single app lets customers collect points across several different local shops, without any shopkeeper having to build anything. Taken beyond the Figma prototype into working HTML/CSS/JS code, with a real QR code and a real camera scanner.

  • Scope: 41 screens/states in Figma, 20 coded pages
  • Key decision: switched from a 4-digit code to the QR code as the main flow, after benchmarking
  • Beyond design: a QR code and camera scanner that really work, dark mode without duplicated screens
Role: UI/UX Designer & DeveloperContext: personal projectTools: Figma, HTML/CSS/JS

Graphic project

Art direction & visual identity

Alongside UI/UX, I also work on visual identity. For Ô Natur'Elle, I drew the logo myself: a single-line portrait of the beautician, designed to embody the softness and elegance of the brand.

Brand identity · Ô Natur'Elle

The Ô Natur'Elle logo — a hand-drawn portrait

Rather than starting from an abstract monogram, I chose to draw the beautician's own face as a single continuous line (single line art), surrounded by floral motifs that echo the salon's natural treatments. The circular stroke and the script lettering of "Ô natur'elle" balance the precision of the portrait with softness.

  • Type: real logo, client project (Ô Natur'Elle)
  • Technique: single-line illustration, drawn by hand
  • Use: brand identity, applied to the website and the Figma prototype

Process

How we work together

Four simple steps, no jargon. You always know where your project stands.

  1. 01

    We get to know each other

    A first free conversation, by phone, video call or over a coffee in Brussels, to understand your business, your customers and what you want to achieve.

  2. 02

    I send you a quote

    What's included, the price and the timeline, in black and white. No surprises along the way.

  3. 03

    I design, you approve

    Research, mock-ups, prototype: I show you the progress at every key stage and we adjust together.

  4. 04

    I deliver, and stay reachable

    You receive the final files. For a website, I take care of putting it online and show you how to use it. The domain name (your web address, around €10 a year) is in your name and belongs to you.

Frequently asked questions

You may be wondering…

Another question? Drop me a message, I reply quickly.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need: a logo isn't priced like a full website. After a first free conversation, I send you a clear, detailed quote with no obligation.

How long does it take?

We set the timeline together in the quote, based on the size of the project and your constraints. From day one, you know what you'll receive and when.

I don't have a logo or brand guidelines — is that a problem?

Not at all. I can create your visual identity before designing the website, so everything is consistent from the start.

Do we need to meet in person?

Whatever suits you. I'm based in Brussels: we can meet in the area, or do everything remotely, by phone, WhatsApp or video call.

Can you also build the website?

Yes, for showcase websites, which I code myself in HTML/CSS/JS. For a more complex application, I prepare mock-ups and a prototype ready to hand over to a developer.

What if I don't know exactly what I want yet?

That's exactly where my job begins. We start by understanding your needs and your customers, and build the right solution together.
