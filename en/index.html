FAQ

You might be wondering…

Another question? Write to me, I reply quickly.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need: a logo isn't priced like a full website. After a first free conversation, I send you a clear, detailed, no-obligation quote.

How long does it take?

We set the schedule together in the quote, based on the size of the project and your constraints. You know from the start when you'll get what.

I don't have a logo or brand guidelines. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I can create your visual identity before the website, so everything fits together from day one.

Do we need to meet in person?

Whatever you prefer. We can meet in and around Brussels, or do everything remotely: phone, WhatsApp or video call.

Can you build the website too, not just design it?

Yes for showcase websites, which I code myself. For a more complex app, I prepare the designs and a prototype ready to hand over to a developer.

What if I don't know exactly what I want yet?

That's exactly where I start. We begin with your business and your customers, and build the right solution together.
